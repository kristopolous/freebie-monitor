# Rewards Glossary

**New-account bonus** — a one-time payout (cash or points) for opening an account and meeting
a requirement, usually a minimum deposit or minimum spend within a set number of days.

**Minimum spend / minimum deposit** — the dollar amount that must be spent or deposited within
the requirement window to qualify for the bonus. Missing the deadline forfeits the bonus
entirely; there is no partial credit for most issuers.

**Maintenance window** — for bank bonuses, the balance often has to stay above the deposit
amount for an additional period (commonly 60-90 days) after the initial deposit, or the bonus
is clawed back.

**5/24 rule** — Chase will not approve most of its own credit cards for someone who has opened
5 or more personal credit cards (any bank) in the past 24 months. This is not written in any
card's terms; it is enforced silently at approval time.

**Account family / once-per-family rule** — some issuers only pay a sign-up bonus once per
"family" of similar cards, even years apart. Amex and Chase both do this. Opening a card that
looks new but is in the same family as one you've had before often pays no bonus at all.

**Annual fee** — recurring yearly charge on some cards. A bonus's real value is its payout
minus the annual fee for the first year, not the payout alone.

**Retention offer** — when calling to cancel a card before the annual fee posts, issuers will
sometimes offer a statement credit or bonus points to keep the account open instead.
